Happy Coin Stack

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

Happy Coin Stack is a single-player casual coin-stacking and merging game. It does not require an account, sign-in, or any personal profile to play. This policy describes what information the app and its third-party services collect, why, and how it is handled.

1 · Information We Collect

Directly from you

  • None. The app has no account, login, profile, or in-app messaging. Your score, best score, and settings are stored only on your own device and are never sent to us.

Automatically, via third-party services

  • Advertising identifiers and standard ad-request data (e.g. device type, general location derived from IP, ad interaction events) — collected by Google AdMob when an ad is shown
  • App usage and gameplay analytics events (e.g. game started, level/merge milestones, session length) — collected by Firebase Analytics
  • Purchase data for optional in-app purchases (product purchased, transaction status) — handled entirely by Google Play Billing; the app never sees or stores your payment card details

2 · What We Do Not Collect

  • Name, email address, or any account/profile information
  • Precise GPS location
  • Payment card or bank details
  • Photos, contacts, or any other device content

3 · Advertising & Monetization

Happy Coin Stack is free to play and supported by advertising and optional in-app purchases:

  • Advertising — interstitial and rewarded video ads are served through Google AdMob. Rewarded ads are always optional and shown only when you choose to watch one (e.g. to continue a run).
  • In-app purchases — an optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase and optional consumable coin packs are available through Google Play Billing. These are entirely optional; the app is fully playable without buying anything.

4 · Third-Party Services

The app integrates the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Firebase Analytics — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Play Billing — policies.google.com/privacy

5 · Children's Privacy

Happy Coin Stack is not directed at children under 13 and is not a "Made for Families" app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising is configured to comply with Google Play's Families and Ads policies, including serving non-personalized ads to any user who is detected or declared to be under the applicable age of consent.

6 · Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data described above is shared only with the third-party services listed in Section 4, solely for the purposes of showing ads, purchase processing, and understanding app usage.

7 · Data Retention & Deletion

Because the app stores gameplay data locally on your device rather than on our servers, uninstalling the app removes that data. Data held by our third-party service providers (AdMob, Firebase, Google Play Billing) is retained and can be deleted according to each provider's own policy — see Section 4 for links, or contact us using the details in Section 9 and we will help route a deletion request.

8 · Data Security

We do not operate our own backend servers for this app — gameplay data stays on your device, and all other data described here is processed directly by Google's AdMob, Firebase, and Play Billing infrastructure under their own security practices.

9 · Changes to This Policy

We may revise this policy from time to time. The current version is always available at this URL. Material changes will be noted by updating the date at the top of the page.
