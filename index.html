Happy Coin Stack — Privacy policy

Updated 23 October 2026

Happy Coin Stack is provided by Tripex. Contact: tripexsupport@gmail.com.

Your game and purchases

The game has no player account. Your stack, score, coin discoveries, campaign progress, preferences and cached ad-removal entitlement are stored on your device. They remain until the app's local data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Google Play processes purchases and may restore eligible purchases using your Google Play account. We do not receive or store your card details. Purchase tokens are processed to acknowledge purchases; the game stores token hashes locally to avoid duplicate delivery.

Remove Ads is an optional one-time purchase that removes automatic ads. Optional rewarded continues remain available. Coin packs and subscriptions are not offered by the current game.

Advertising and choices

When ads are available, Google's mobile advertising SDK may process device and advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from network information, app interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention and service operation. Consent information is requested through Google's User Messaging Platform. Where a privacy-choice entry is required, Settings provides Privacy choices. If consent or advertising services are unavailable, you can continue playing without an ad. Rewarded continuation is optional. Campaign stage transitions and retries do not show ads.

Analytics

Firebase Analytics is included in the Android build with collection disabled. Play-session diagnostics used during local testing are stored locally and are not uploaded by the game's diagnostic logger.

Retention and deletion

Local game information is kept on your device. Clearing app data removes the local stack, preferences and discoveries; it does not cancel or refund Google Play purchases. Contact us for privacy inquiries or questions about data processed by our service providers. Google controls retention and deletion for information processed by its advertising, analytics and purchase services under its own policies.

Children and audience

The game does not ask for your name, contact list, camera, microphone or precise location, and does not offer an account-creation flow. Please contact us if you have questions about a child's use of the game or information processed by our service providers.

Policy updates

We may update this policy when features or service providers change. The policy date identifies the current version.
